Meet the Creator & Story

"Hi! I am Priya, and I started Cozy Loops to share the therapeutic art of crochet. Every single plushie, tote bag, and tulip flower is crocheted by hand, loops over loops, ensuring top quality, extreme softness, and durability."

Unlike mass-produced plastic toys, our amigurumi plushies use premium cotton yarn, safety lock eyes, and plush stuffing so they remain your softest companion forever.
